# Credits

Glory of Thornwick is an original game. Its story, code, music and scenery were made for it; no Sierra assets are used.

## Character sprites

The characters are built from the [Liberated Pixel Cup](https://lpc.opengameart.org/) sprites, via the
[Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator).
They are used under the licences listed for each file (CC-BY-SA 3.0, CC-BY 3.0/4.0, OGA-BY 3.0, GPL 2.0/3.0 or CC0),
and were recoloured and combined for this game by `scripts/lpc.ts`. The combined sheets in `src/art/lpc` are
shared under the same terms.

Artists: Ahmad3366, AntumDeluge, Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), bluecarrot16, Charles Sanchez (CharlesGabriel), dalonedrau, Daniel Eddeland (daneeklu), DarkwallLKE, Durrani, Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), ElizaWy, ElizaWy; walk and down by JaidynReiman, Evert, gr3yh47, Inboxninja, JaidynReiman, Joe White, Johannes Sjölund (wulax), Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Luke Mehl, Manuel Riecke (MrBeast), Matthew Krohn (makrohn), Matthew Krohn (Makrohn), Michael Whitlock (bigbeargames), MuffinElZangano, Napsio (Vitruvian Studio), Nila122, Pierre Vigier (pvigier), Sander Frenken (castelonia), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), thecilekli, TheraHedwig, Tuomo Untinen (reemax), unknown artist, William.Thomsponj.
